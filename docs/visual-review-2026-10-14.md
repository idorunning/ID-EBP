# IDEBP visual and source review — 14 October 2026

## What changed

The original live view used an unloaded Inter declaration, serif headings, an undersized index symbol and unbranded source initials. The revised design loads Public Sans from the same origin, uses upright headings throughout and gives the IDEBP wordmark a clear typographic treatment. The favicon is a crisp SVG.

White, navy (#132C45), blue (#23588A), pale blue (#EDF4FA), ink (#172B3A) and restrained copper focus accents (#A65A32) form the palette. Search, navigation, cards, filters and profiles share the same type scale, borders, spacing and button treatment. Official source identities appear in consistent banner areas; colours do not represent research quality.

## Editorial refresh

Twenty source listings across seven organisation countries. Eight added: Police Science Dr, Cambridge Centre for Evidence-Based Policing, CrimeSolutions, Campbell Crime and Justice, ANZSEBP, American SEBP, Dutch SEBP and New Zealand Police's EBP Centre. Descriptions are AI-drafted from official source pages, checked against those pages and deliberately brief. They describe available material, not effectiveness or quality.

Seventeen original source logo files are self-hosted. The College's two records and CEPOL use ordinary text identities because explicit permission is required for third-party logo use. Marks are not reconstructed. Original source URLs, asset URLs, checked dates and caveats are recorded in the three research manifests. No photographs were copied.

Corrections include the current GPD collaborators, its subscription access, CrimeSolutions' discontinued updates, the independent status of CCEBP, Welsh YEF material and selected POP translations. A translated publication does not imply that an entire collection is translated. Founder ownership of Thinking About Policing remains disclosed.

## Verification

Visually reviewed desktop, tablet and mobile homepages, the directory and a branded source profile. Checked loaded font faces, upright headings, logo rendering, keyboard focus, and horizontal fit at 320, 390, 820 and desktop widths. Browser acceptance checks cover filtering, interface translations, saved-source persistence, registration preview and admin preview isolation. Worker tests check binary PNG and WOFF2 delivery alongside existing routing and access protections.

The production account service is still separate from this design preview. No accounts, registrations, email messages or public recommendations are claimed to be live.
